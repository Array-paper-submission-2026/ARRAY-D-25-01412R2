Areas of work include gender equality, anti-corruption, elections, parliament, civil society, peaceful conflict mitigation, and youth civic engagement. Supported the funding of key civic projects including: Children Parliament, NGOs Directory in Yemen, First Educational Yemeni Cartoon, Children Library, and Women Income Generation project and Youth Community Participation. Was awarded the Foreign Service National award of the Year 2003 for high achievement in the outreach to the civil society and positive outcome and impact of the program.
