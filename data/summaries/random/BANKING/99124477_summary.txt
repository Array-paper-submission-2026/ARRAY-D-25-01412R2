In conjuction with the current functionality of HUB, HSBC has decided to include additional functionality in the OHCB to meet One HSBC solution. The whole transmission system consists of two parts: 1) The sending part on Legacy Warehouse Country side 2) The receiving part on S&D IW side For each sending system exists in the S&D IW one dedicated receiving System. Because up to 12 tables are fed, there are also 12 interfaces, each responsible for one target table.
