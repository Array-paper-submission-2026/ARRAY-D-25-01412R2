After Franklin Pierce Law Center integrated with UNH, I developed the plan for and administered the transition from a predominately Novell-based infrastructure to a predominately Microsoft-based infrastructure, including transitioning our e-mail platform from Novell GroupWise to Microsoft Exchange. Duties ranged from day-to-day troubleshooting to implementing new technologies, such as the first ever Apple Airport WiFi network and roaming iBook lab. Appliances and applications: Drupal, Expression Engine, Aerohive, Bluesocket Wireless Controller, Barracuda Anti-Spam Appliance, Barracuda Web Filter, BarracudaWare, SonicWall NSA (including VPN), 3Com NBX, Avaya, Cisco PIX, Cisco Network Assistant, Blackberry Enterprise Server, VMWare ESX, VMWare Fusion, Pharos Uniprint, NTop, Nagios, NagiosQL, Icinga, Cacti, RealChat, MRBS, WebCalendar.
