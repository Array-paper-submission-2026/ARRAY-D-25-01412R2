I joined the Ministry of Education in 1982 and was posted at the R.M.Bailey Senior High School where I remained for seventeen and a half years. I served as Head of the Guidance Department from 1991 to 1999 until my redeployment to Special Services Section. In 2003,after visiting the school system in Dallas Texas and learning about their Employee Assistance Program, I designed and implemented a portion of the program. I enjoyed my role as Senior Education Officer and would like to be a part of taking EAP to the next level.
