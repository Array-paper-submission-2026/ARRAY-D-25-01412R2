Programmed a utility in VB.NET to assist desktop technicians in automatically adding machines to AD groups and SCCM collections for deployment of MSI/App-V based installations, significantly reducing the amount of time needed to deploy applications to groups of machines. Created a utility in VB.NET to automate the updating of distribution points, creation of new collections (with appropriate rights), creation of AD groups, and advertisement creation, allowing the Enterprise Client Systems team to quickly respond to new deployment requests. Assisted in the design, testing, and implementation of the Windows 7 image for Intermountain Healthcare, migrating from a custom imaging solution to OSD in SCCM.
